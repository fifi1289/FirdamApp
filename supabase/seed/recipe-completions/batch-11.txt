# Batch 11 — dinner versions of Syrian, French and American dishes written earlier, plus new American dinners

@ 122005d1-350a-47ba-b519-4b24ca8be0dd | American Halal BBQ Beef Brisket Dinner Platter
i: 1.8 kg halal beef brisket | 2 tbsp smoked paprika | 2 tbsp brown sugar | 1 tbsp garlic powder | 1 tbsp onion powder | 1 tsp black pepper | 250 ml tomato ketchup | 3 tbsp apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp molasses | 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce | 400 g cabbage; shredded | 4 tbsp mayonnaise | 600 g potatoes; cut into wedges | 2 tbsp vegetable oil | salt to taste
s: Rub the brisket with the paprika, half the sugar, garlic and onion powders, pepper and salt and rest 1 hour. | Wrap tightly in foil with 100 ml water and cook at 140°C for 5 hours until tender. | Simmer the ketchup, vinegar, molasses, worcestershire and remaining sugar for 10 minutes, then brush over the brisket and roast uncovered at 220°C for 15 minutes. | Roast the oiled, salted potato wedges at 220°C for 35 minutes and toss the cabbage with mayonnaise and salt. | Slice the brisket across the grain and serve with sauce, slaw and wedges.

@ 2ddcaf0a-ff11-4a5e-ab8f-1092142de7dc | American Halal Chicken Pot Pie Dinner with Flaky Crust
i: 600 g halal chicken breast; diced | 2 pieces potatoes; diced | 2 pieces carrots; diced | 150 g frozen peas | 1 pieces onion; diced | 50 g butter | 50 g plain flour | 500 ml halal chicken stock | 150 ml milk | 1 tsp dried thyme | 500 g shortcrust pastry | 1 pieces egg; beaten | salt to taste | black pepper to taste
s: Cook the chicken, onion, potatoes and carrots in the butter for 8 minutes. | Stir in the flour, then the stock, milk and thyme, and simmer for 10 minutes until thick and the potatoes are tender. | Add the peas, season and cool slightly. | Line a pie dish with half the pastry, fill and cover with the rest, crimping the edge and brushing with egg. | Bake at 200°C for 35 minutes until golden.

@ 38658965-a0d5-4e43-a35f-eed7a12e4a4b | American Halal Blackened Salmon Dinner with Rice and Broccoli
i: 3 pieces salmon fillets | 2 tsp smoked paprika | 1 tsp garlic powder | 1 tsp onion powder | 1 tsp dried thyme | 1 tsp dried oregano | 0.5 tsp cayenne pepper | 30 g butter | 1 tbsp vegetable oil | 300 g long-grain rice | 400 g broccoli florets | 1 pieces lemon | salt to taste
s: Mix the spices, herbs and salt and press onto the flesh side of the salmon. | Cook the rice and steam the broccoli for 5 minutes. | Heat a cast-iron pan until very hot, add the oil and butter and cook the salmon spice-side down for 3 minutes until blackened. | Turn and cook 3 minutes more. | Serve with rice, broccoli and lemon.

@ 3df89a13-38d9-4e10-9503-cb3a35f2a504 | American Halal Glazed Ham-Style Turkey Roast Dinner
i: 1.5 kg halal turkey breast roast | 4 tbsp brown sugar | 3 tbsp honey | 2 tbsp dijon mustard | 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar | 0.5 tsp ground cloves | 800 g potatoes; quartered | 400 g green beans | 3 tbsp olive oil | 30 g butter | salt to taste | black pepper to taste
s: Rub the turkey with 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper and roast at 180°C for 60 minutes. | Mix the sugar, honey, mustard, vinegar and cloves into a glaze. | Brush the turkey with the glaze every 10 minutes for the last 30 minutes of roasting, until sticky and the centre reaches 74°C. | Roast the potatoes in the remaining oil alongside for 50 minutes and boil the beans for 5 minutes, tossing them with butter. | Rest the turkey 15 minutes, slice and serve with the vegetables.

@ 4eae34da-b7c3-4f84-b8fb-1b8cf5ef93ab | American Halal St. Louis BBQ Pork-Free Ribs Dinner
i: 1.5 kg halal beef short ribs | 2 tbsp smoked paprika | 2 tbsp brown sugar | 1 tbsp garlic powder | 1 tsp ground cumin | 1 tsp black pepper | 200 ml tomato ketchup | 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar | 2 tbsp honey | 1 tbsp worcestershire sauce | 4 pieces corn on the cob | 30 g butter | salt to taste
s: Rub the ribs with the paprika, half the sugar, garlic powder, cumin, pepper and salt. | Wrap in foil with a splash of water and bake at 150°C for 3 hours until tender. | Simmer the ketchup, vinegar, honey, worcestershire and remaining sugar for 10 minutes. | Unwrap, brush with the sauce and roast at 220°C for 15 minutes, basting twice. | Boil the corn for 8 minutes, butter it and serve with the ribs.

@ 4f085436-811d-4996-a366-035856f0f6dd | American Halal Jambalaya Dinner with Chicken and Beef Sausage
i: 500 g halal chicken thighs; diced | 250 g halal beef sausages; sliced | 1 pieces onion; diced | 1 pieces green bell pepper; diced | 2 pieces celery stalks; diced | 3 cloves garlic | 400 g chopped tomatoes | 300 g long-grain rice | 700 ml halal chicken stock | 2 tsp smoked paprika | 1 tsp dried thyme | 0.5 tsp cayenne pepper | 2 pieces bay leaves | 2 tbsp vegetable oil | 3 pieces spring onions | salt to taste
s: Brown the sausage and chicken in the oil and remove. | Soften the onion, pepper, celery and garlic for 6 minutes, add the spices. | Add the tomatoes, stock, bay leaves, rice and the meats and bring to a simmer. | Cover and cook on low for 20 minutes until the rice is tender. | Rest 5 minutes and serve with spring onions.

@ 9e2ed159-f6db-497e-b894-f768e393a8df | American Halal Roasted Turkey Dinner with Gravy and Stuffing
i: 2.5 kg halal turkey crown or breast and thighs | 80 g butter; softened | 2 tsp dried sage | 2 tsp dried thyme | 300 g stale bread; cubed | 1 pieces onion; chopped | 2 pieces celery stalks; chopped | 600 ml halal chicken stock | 1 kg potatoes | 150 ml milk | 2 tbsp plain flour | 300 g cranberry sauce | salt to taste | black pepper to taste
s: Rub the turkey with half the butter, herbs, salt and pepper and roast at 180°C for about 2 hours, until the thickest part reaches 74°C, then rest 30 minutes. | Soften the onion and celery in 20 g butter, mix with the bread, herbs and 250 ml stock and bake in a dish for 35 minutes. | Boil and mash the potatoes with the milk, remaining butter and salt. | Stir the flour into the roasting juices, whisk in the remaining stock and simmer for gravy. | Carve and serve with stuffing, mash, gravy and cranberry sauce.
