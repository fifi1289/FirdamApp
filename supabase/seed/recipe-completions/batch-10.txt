@ 53e0d8e3-603c-48a6-9f0e-65eafe82efa6 | Algerian Halal Mthawem Garlic Chicken Stew Dinner
i: 800 g halal chicken pieces | 300 g halal beef mince | 1 pieces onion; grated | 12 cloves garlic; crushed | 2 tsp ground cumin | 1 tsp paprika | 1 tsp black pepper | 200 g cooked chickpeas | 1 handful fresh parsley; chopped | 1 pieces egg | 3 tbsp olive oil | 1 pieces lemon | salt to taste
s: Mix the beef with a third of the garlic, half the cumin, half the parsley, the egg and salt and roll into small meatballs. | Cook the chicken with the onion, another third of the garlic, the remaining cumin, paprika, pepper, oil and salt for 10 minutes. | Add 700 ml water and the chickpeas and simmer for 25 minutes. | Add the meatballs and simmer for 15 minutes, then stir in the last of the garlic and cook 5 minutes. | Finish with parsley and lemon and serve with bread.
